Native iOS GPS simulation controller.

GeoShift is a lightweight desktop instrument built in Rust (Tauri v2) that dispatches ephemeral coordinates directly to Apple's developer instrumentation daemon over physical USB — no jailbreak, no browser bloat, and instant one-click satellite restoration.

iPhone setup in three steps

Works with stock iOS devices over Lightning or USB-C.

Step 1

Pair device over USB

Connect your iPhone via cable, unlock the screen, and confirm Trust This Computer with your device passcode.

Step 2

Enable Developer Mode

On iOS 16+, toggle Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode once so Apple's DVT instrumentation daemon accepts local overrides.

Step 3

Dispatch or restore instantly

Drop a pin anywhere on the map to update system-wide telemetry, or click Restore Hardware GPS to hand control straight back to native satellites.
